IDMB - Banking, Wallets & Data Analytics Infrastructure

IDMB is the modern infrastructure layer for finance. Banks, fintechs and operators use IDMB to ship financial products on a single, trusted platform that combines core banking, embedded wallets and real-time data analytics.

Banking infrastructure

Production-grade core banking APIs, double-entry ledger, accounts, payments and card issuing - composable services that compress the path to a regulated financial product from quarters to weeks.

Wallets infrastructure

Multi-currency, multi-tenant wallet infrastructure with stored-value accounts, instant transfers, top-ups, payouts and full lifecycle controls - engineered for high-throughput consumer and B2B use cases.

Data analytics

Stream-native analytics over the IDMB ledger: customer 360, risk and fraud signals, regulatory reporting and revenue intelligence - from the same source of truth that powers your products.
